At Organiyum.in, customer satisfaction is important to us. This policy explains how returns and refunds are handled.

1. Returns

We accept returns only if the product is damaged, defective, or incorrect at the time of delivery.

  • Return request must be raised within 48 hours of delivery
  • Product must be unused and in original packaging
  • Proof of purchase is required

2. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are non-returnable:

  • Perishable goods
  • Opened or used products
  • Items purchased on sale or discount

3. Refunds

Once the return is approved and received, refunds will be processed within 7–10 business days.

Refunds will be credited to the original payment method.

4. Replacement

If eligible, we may offer a replacement instead of a refund depending on product availability.

5. Shipping Costs

Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.
